GMC Square Body

GMC Square Body trucks were produced from 1973 through 1987 and are easily recognized by their boxy, squared-off body design. They were available in different configurations, including two-wheel drive and four-wheel drive. Square Bodies are popular today because of their classic appearance, simple mechanical design, and large aftermarket for restoration and customization.

Ford F-Series

The Ford F-Series included several models designed for different levels of hauling and towing. The F-100 and F-150 became especially well known as everyday pickup trucks. Ford trucks from this period are often restored to their original appearance or modified with modern parts while keeping their classic body style.

"The Ford F-150 model history is a tale of constant innovation. Over the years, Ford has introduced numerous F-150 models, each with unique features tailored to meet the needs of different drivers. The F-150 history includes landmark models like the 1997 F-150, which marked a major redesign that added a more aerodynamic shape and a range of new engines." JC Lewis Ford Aug 15, 2024

Dodge Ram/Power Wagon

The Dodge Ram name became associated with Dodge's pickup trucks in the early 1980s. These trucks featured distinctive styling and were available with different engines and drivetrain options. Older Dodge trucks are appreciated for their unique designs and their place in the history of American pickup trucks.
